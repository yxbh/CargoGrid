# Working on Cargo-Grid

- Work only in this project unless the user explicitly authorizes another checkout. Preserve existing work, the MIT notice and the independently authored generator. Do not vendor workbench code, private references, slicer profiles or restricted third-party meshes; local reference inspection does not grant redistribution or design rights.
- Python source and named parameters own design intent. STEP, STL, 3MF, PNG and viewer data are derived. Keep ordinary generated work under ignored output directories; only intentionally documented, source-derived overviews and per-variant thumbnails under `docs/images/` belong in source control.
- Preserve standard compatibility invariants: millimeters, southwest body-corner origin, Z=0 underside, 60 mm unit size, 13 mm tile thickness and original roofed joints. Custom unit size scales cell spacing and nominal tile/X interface geometry in its local plane; thickness independently drives body and insertion depth. Keep fit allowances and physical comfort radii absolute. Full-height tile-edge joints are explicitly experimental with the documented thin-web limitation.
- Full 10 mm round holes are on by default with full scope; `--no-holes`/`hole_diameter=None` is the explicit solid-web path and interior scope remains available. Roof support stays off by default. Enabled roof support targets retained west/south female roofs, with critical coverage and an intentional PETG-base/PLA-interface zero-contact workflow: top gap 0, independent support layers off, dense spacing 0, at least two top interface layers and build-plate-only off. Preserve all five process markers. Reject unsuitable/same-material roof requests; do not infer chemical compatibility from display names.
- Every generated Bambu plate defaults to automatic Convenience Mode (`Auto For Match`) without physical map caches. Custom mapping is explicit only. Initial support-foot expansion stays native automatic unless requested otherwise. The zero-contact PETG/PLA workflow preserves its measured 0.40 mm XY side-clearance safeguard; do not apply it to unrelated workflows or change bridge, threshold, cooling, speed or prime/flush settings to hide contact problems.
- H2D dual-safe catalogues use common reach X25..325, Y0..320, Z<=320, a further 5 mm model inset and the shared default model-clearance constant, with family-coherent named plates. Keep the 5x5 tile isolated and explicitly left-nozzle-only; do not scale it or let automatic mapping span both exclusive zones. Validate support/brim/tower bounds separately from visible model bounds.
- Full-hole 2x1 roof-support jobs intentionally occupy the three female-edge round cutouts at (0,30), (30,0) and (90,0) with removable support during printing. Explain removal access; do not claim these holes are open during printing or permanently blocked after removal. Keep protected X openings clear.
- Preserve CLI/API names, manifest semantics, quantities, overwrite refusal and unsupported-combination errors. Roof-plus-stack and mixed-catalogue roof support remain unsupported. A documentation change must not change geometry or public behavior.
- The unreleased `lock-90` family is replaced by `vertical-tile-bracket`; do not restore an alias or obsolete upright walls. Keep the original floor/wall 1x2, 2x1 and 2x2 variants plus only the approved shallow floor 1x1 -> wall 1x2 and floor 2x1 -> wall 2x2 variants. `nx` is base/wall width, `ny` is floor depth and optional `panel_height_cells` is wall height; omit/match it for stable original identities. All five use the accepted two-way panel post: no seating-plane root flare, 0.08 mm inset straight stem, 0.1 mm transition and exact unchanged final R2 tip, with reinforcement behind the seat. Default wall tiles have the underside outward; top-outward placement is Z180 then X-90 and every adjoining wall tile must share that orientation because left/right handedness reverses. Preserve the measured nominal insertion interference and keep physical fit pending. Every bracket uses R3 at the analogous thick front-to-slope transition; other R2 free-body/R1 bearing-lip rounds, the internal bearing corner, exact floor X regions, separate ordinary tiles and nominal zero-gap strip remain. Original brackets use their computed retangent rear-face-down pose; shallow Bambu exports are Y=-90 side-down with object-scoped normal Auto. Support reaches both shallow X mating regions and requires removal/fit review.
- Keep `ramp` as the approved floor-to-mat transition: rise follows tile thickness, the finished body run stays 50 mm, and width uses an integer number of selected unit cells. `ramp_join` / `--ramp-join` selects female (the backward-compatible default) or male shared original roofed tile-edge geometry, repeated once per cell. The female ramp receives a north male tile edge and extends in positive Y. Male tabs project toward negative Y beyond the 50 mm body run by `0.1 * pitch`; rotate Z=180 to join a south female tile edge, or Z=90 for a west female tile edge. Preserve R2 side/nose rounds, the broad R32 shelf-to-slope transition, the R1 underside mating transition and the high joining rim. Cap the shelf radius only as needed to retain 2 mm of high flat land on thicker custom ramps; do not scale the radius with unit size. Resolve shelf and nose rounds in section before side-cap rounds. Full-height ramp joints, corner ramps, separate male adapters, hole patterns and copied third-party contours remain unsupported.
- Keep the eight `vertical-stop` variants at explicit 1x1, 1x2, 2x1 and 2x2 bases crossed with H60/H120 shoulder heights. They are full-width filled wedges with no wall holes, open ribs, panel plugs or ledges; 2x1/H120 uses the natural wedge, not a raised 45-degree toe. Preserve the coupled R2 blend on every free exterior edge and exact X plugs/roots. Object-scoped normal Auto is intentional; 1x1/H120 and 2x1/H120 support can enter the mounting region and require removal/fit review.
- Keep both `lock-45` variants as full-width filled wedges, not the earlier open-backed wall/web construction. Preserve the 45-degree cargo face, 4.1 mm toe, 6 mm horizontal cap, R2 coupled across all 18 free body edges, exact X connectors/roots, unchanged outer bounds and X=-135-degree Bambu pose. Do not add object-scoped support; the bounded native OFF/Auto checks were support-free.
- Keep `Rod` and `RodBrace` as narrow millimetre parameter types, not universal `Accessory` fields or tile/X interfaces. The catalogue adds only 120/240 mm above-mat rods and 60/120 mm-centre nominal Ø10 braces. Rods keep the fixed Ø10 shaft, Ø18 x 3.2 collar, R2 top and insertion depth `tile_thickness_mm-1`; standard insertion is 12 mm with an 11 mm straight region and 1 mm lead. Braces keep physical spacing, printable labels, 78/138 x 18 x 6.4 mm bounds, R9 ends, R1 top and 0.4 mm bottom chamfer; bores have 5.6 mm straight depth and 0.4 mm leads at both mouths. Keep the parametric bore option without presenting custom diameters as additional official variants. Unit size scales none of these physical dimensions; custom thickness changes only rod insertion.
- Bambu rods use Y=+90 horizontal placement and object-scoped normal Auto; braces stay flat with bores along Z and no object support. Source STEP/STL/core 3MF keep upright rods and flat braces. Preserve existing family identities and group the four new designs on `Rods and upper braces`. Nominal Ø10 rod/peg-to-mat fit is user-reported only for PETG at 0.8 mm nozzle / 0.32 mm layers in the horizontal pose. Nominal Ø10 brace bores fitting nominal Ø10 rod shafts are also user-reported, but the tested brace spacing or spacings were not specified; do not infer other bore fits, spacing-specific results or strength. Bags rest on the mat; brace friction is not a positive height lock and may slide or jam. No hooks or load/crash rating.
- Bambu exports apply plate X=180 body-down, each original bracket's computed retangent rear-face-down X rotation, shallow-bracket Y=-90, per-design normal-stop broad-rear-face-down and angled-stop X=-135 before fit checks and packing; source STEP/STL/core 3MF retain model orientation. Record exact transforms and artifact-specific applied flags.
- Use R3 on original-style edge/corner bodies and straight rail/support bodies, R3/R2 on straight rail windows, R3 on every bracket's thick front-to-slope transition, other mixed R2/R1 bracket rounds, R2 on plate/angled-stop bodies, and R2 on normal stops/ramps. Rail ends use the tested per-variant coupled/sequential body and window-rim radii in `accessories.py`; do not replace them with one blanket construction. Apply shared parameterized tile joints/X connectors after rounding nonfunctional bodies. The separate support-rail dovetail remains fixed physical geometry. Keep experimental full-height rounding separate and do not relax tolerances for kernel failures.
- CLI names encode units and meaning: `--unit-size-mm`, `--tile-thickness-mm`, `--build-width-mm`, `--build-depth-mm`, `--build-height-mm`, `--stop-height-mm`, named `*-mm` gaps/diameters, and explicit `--width-cells`/`--depth-cells`/`--length-cells` counts. Do not restore `--grid-pitch-mm`, `--tile-height-mm`, positional `--cells`/`--footprint` or other alpha spellings. Keep ramp callers on `--width-cells` because run stays fixed at 50 mm. `--copy-count` repeats a design; layout width/depth describe the assembly. Manifest/API `pitch`/`height` keys remain stable.
- Read the relevant source/tests before editing. Validate generated geometry and exports, not just constants: body validity/count, positive volume, actual dimensions, interfaces, holes, layout and mesh topology. Do not relax tolerances, mesh-repair budgets or expected results merely to make a check pass.
- Use `pyproject.toml` and normal `uv sync` with configured feeds. Keep `uv.lock`, environments, caches and runtime data ignored; do not add machine-specific registry URLs. Documentation-only rendering must not add runtime dependencies.
- Use relative repository paths in maintained docs/examples. Keep prose paragraphs and list items on one source line. Keep guidance evergreen: no session histories, one-off success counts, private paths or links to ignored study artifacts.
- Write for one hobbyist helping another: direct, practical and modest. Keep the README to an evergreen introduction, useful commands and operation-specific limits; put validation history and pending development checks in existing maintainer notes instead. Keep useful measurements and caveats, but explain them once in ordinary language. Avoid marketing copy, corporate workflow jargon, forced enthusiasm and strings of words such as “robust”, “seamless”, “comprehensive”, “validated”, “guarded” or “explicit” when a concrete statement is clearer.
- Run the smallest relevant tests first, plus `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run ruff format --check .` and `git diff --check`. On the documented workstation, run the full portable suite with `uv run pytest -q -n 12 --dist loadfile -m "not native and not reference"`; use fewer workers on smaller machines. CI runs the tests not marked `slow` on every pull request under a per-test duration budget and runs the `slow` tier weekly; follow `tests/README.md` for representative sites, local clipped queries and shared read-only fixtures. Keep native Bambu and local-reference checks serial with `uv run pytest -q` and set `CARGO_GRID_BAMBU`/`CARGO_GRID_REFERENCE` only for resources supplied for that run. Unavailable external checks remain skips, not passes.
- For packaging changes run `uv build` and `uv run python tools/check_distributions.py dist` in a fresh build directory. Verify a clean wheel install separately from an editable checkout. Prepare authorized source releases from reviewed tracked files, not a working-directory ZIP; see `docs/release-checklist.md`.
- When external CAD tools are requested, use the approved current workbench interpreter from this design root, generate an explicit project-relative STEP and inspect that same STEP. Prefer an actually available maintained CAD viewer; otherwise report the capability gap and use its documented fallback. Do not mix viewer reference systems or substitute a demo model.
- Render documentation from committed generator APIs, record the source/tool revisions and exact inventory scope, and check coverage, labels, links and image size. Do not imply exhaustive coverage of unbounded parametric dimensions.
- Core 3MF is geometry; native-compatible 3MF contains diagnostic, unsliced project metadata, not calibrated factory profiles or print approval. GUI actions, printer operations, package/model publication, commits and pushes require explicit authorization.
- Keep geometric checks, actual slicer observations and physical results separate. Fit, support removal, flatness, strength and service suitability remain unverified until applicable observations are supplied and recorded. Never infer physical outcomes from silence or a valid solid.
